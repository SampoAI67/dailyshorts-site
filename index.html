On this day, every day

DailyShorts

A personal, single-channel tool that makes one short "On This Day" history video a day, styled as a 1920s newsreel, and delivers it to its owner's own YouTube channel and Google Drive.

What it does

  • Picks a light-hearted event that happened on the next day's date, from Wikipedia's "On this day" pages.
  • Writes a short script whose every factual line quotes its source.
  • Builds an original animated video from public-domain and CC BY archive images, with full credits in the description.
  • Places the finished video in the owner's Google Drive and, once enabled, uploads it to the owner's YouTube channel as a private, scheduled video that the owner reviews before it goes live.

Who uses it

Only its owner, on their own channel and Drive. It is not offered to other users, does not access anyone else's account and does not collect personal data.
